Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers about working together, testing options, and what to expect from clinical naturopathic coaching.

How does nutritional therapy differ from seeing my primary physician? ▼
Nutritional therapy and clinical naturopathy do not replace conventional medical diagnosis. Instead, we work upstream: investigating the foundational metabolic, microbial, and lifestyle drivers behind your chronic symptoms. We partner alongside your medical care, using personalized food protocols, functional testing, and mucosal repair to help your body self-regulate.
What is included in the 1:1 5-Phase Coaching Protocol? ▼
The 5-Phase Protocol is a comprehensive 4-month partnership. It includes an in-depth initial health timeline investigation (75 mins), bi-weekly review consultations, personalized anti-inflammatory dietary plans, optional functional lab recommendations (GI-MAP, hormone, micronutrient), and continuous chat support between sessions.
Do I need to live in the UK to work with you? ▼
Not at all. Houleye works with clients across the globe, primarily in the United States, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and across Europe. All 1:1 sessions and workshops are conducted securely via Zoom with flexible timezone availability.
What happens during the complimentary 30-minute Clarity Call? ▼
This call is a pressure-free, confidential opportunity to discuss your health history, current challenges, and goals. Houleye will assess whether your symptoms align with her root-cause methodology and outline what a personalized roadmap would look like.
